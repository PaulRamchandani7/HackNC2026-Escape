CODE: ADRIFT

Lost at sea. Trapped aboard. Code your way home.

You wake up alone on a drifting ship. Its systems are failing, its doors are locked, and a storm is closing in. The only way to survive is to restore the ship's systems—one line of Python at a time.

How to play

  1. Wake up aboard the drifting cargo ship and follow the emergency broadcast.
  2. Open the only available compartment on the deck plan.
  3. Each terminal is a real LeetCode problem. The brief and tests are original. The title and link come from LeetCode.
  4. Run checks the samples. Submit runs hidden tests on the server. A room opens only after that verdict is accepted.
  5. Restore five systems — quarters, engines, radio, navigation, and the bridge — before the storm takes the ship.
